Candidate notice and consent v1 approved

The default text for new roles. Candidates read it and agree before the scored call.

Northwind Freight uses Warmcall for the first interview for this role. You'll talk with an AI buyer for about 7 minutes. Warmcall scores three skills: discovery, handling doubt and getting a next step. It doesn't score your voice, accent or face.

A person on our hiring team reviews every result and makes the decision. You can ask for extra time, captions, a text version or a person to interview you instead.
